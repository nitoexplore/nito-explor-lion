NITO EXPLOR

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 September 2026

NITO EXPLOR (PTY) LTD respects the privacy of customers and visitors and is committed to handling personal information responsibly and in accordance with applicable South African privacy requirements.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the nature of the enquiry or booking, NITO EXPLOR may collect information such as:

  • Name and surname.
  • Telephone number.
  • Email address.
  • Booking date and experience information.
  • Number of visitors.
  • Payment and transaction information necessary to confirm or administer a booking.

2. Why We Use Personal Information

Personal information may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries.
  • Process and administer bookings.
  • Confirm payments.
  • Communicate important booking information.
  • Provide customer service.
  • Maintain business and transaction records.
  • Meet applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

3. Data Minimisation

NITO EXPLOR aims to collect only information that is reasonably necessary for the relevant business purpose.

4. Payment Information

Payment information is used for purposes connected with processing and confirming transactions.

Customers should never send PINs, CVV numbers, passwords or complete card details through ordinary email, WhatsApp, SMS or social media.

NITO EXPLOR should not retain card security information after a card transaction has been processed.

5. Sharing Personal Information

Personal information will not be sold to third parties.

Information may be shared where reasonably necessary to provide services, process payments, comply with legal requirements or protect the rights and safety of NITO EXPLOR, visitors and other persons.

6. Security

NITO EXPLOR takes reasonable steps to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

7. Data Accuracy

Customers should provide accurate and current information and notify NITO EXPLOR if information needs to be corrected.

8. Retention

Personal information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including legitimate business, accounting, legal and regulatory requirements.
