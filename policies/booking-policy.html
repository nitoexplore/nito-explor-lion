NITO EXPLOR

Booking Policy

Effective date: 22 September 2026

This policy explains how visitors can make and confirm bookings for NITO EXPLOR wildlife and visitor experiences.

1. Booking by Phone

Bookings are currently handled directly by telephone. Customers can contact NITO EXPLOR on 073 067 8577 during operating hours.

Our operating hours are Monday to Saturday, 08:00 to 17:00.

2. Booking Information

Customers may be asked to provide information necessary to arrange the visit, including:

  • Customer name and surname.
  • Contact telephone number.
  • Preferred visit date.
  • Number of visitors.
  • Selected experience or activity.
  • Any relevant requirements communicated during booking.

3. Booking Confirmation

A booking is only considered confirmed once NITO EXPLOR has confirmed availability and the required payment or payment arrangement has been completed.

Customers should not travel to the park based solely on an enquiry or telephone conversation that has not resulted in a confirmed booking.

4. Availability

Availability is subject to the capacity of the park, experience schedules, operational requirements, weather conditions and animal welfare considerations.

5. Prices

Prices may vary according to the experience, number of visitors, applicable promotions and other booking conditions.

The applicable price will be communicated to the customer before payment is requested.

6. Changes to a Booking

Customers should contact NITO EXPLOR as soon as possible if they need to change the date, number of visitors or experience associated with a booking.

Changes are subject to availability and may be subject to the applicable cancellation or booking conditions.

7. Contact
