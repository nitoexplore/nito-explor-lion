NITO EXPLOR

Children & Minors

Effective date: 22 September 2026

NITO EXPLOR welcomes families where the selected experience is suitable for children. Safety remains the responsibility of the accompanying adult.

1. Adult Supervision

Children and minors must remain under the direct supervision of a responsible parent, guardian or accompanying adult during the visit.

2. Safety Rules

Adults accompanying children must ensure that children follow all NITO EXPLOR safety rules and staff instructions.

3. Experience Restrictions

Certain experiences or areas may have age, height or other safety restrictions.

Customers should confirm applicable requirements when making their booking.

4. Responsibility of Accompanying Adults

Parents, guardians and accompanying adults remain responsible for supervising children and ensuring that their conduct does not create a risk to the child, other visitors, staff or animals.
